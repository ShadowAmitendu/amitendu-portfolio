## S.A.P. Bootcamp Experience

From August 2025 to January 2026, I attended a 6-month SAP ABAP Bootcamp at Techno India Group in Salt Lake City, West Bengal.

This was my introduction to the world of SAP and enterprise software development. During the bootcamp, I learned how business applications are developed inside SAP systems and got hands-on experience with ABAP programming.

## What I Learned

![A view of the room we were sitting in!](/images/blog/2026-10-15-bootcamp-of-sap.webp "A view of the room we were sitting in!")

I started with the fundamentals of ABAP (Advanced Business Application Programming), including:

- Writing simple ABAP programs
- Working with reports and internal tables
- Understanding program flow and data handling
- Learning basic debugging techniques

I also explored Object-Oriented ABAP (OOABAP), where I learned about classes, objects, methods, and reusable code structures used in modern SAP development.

Alongside programming, I was introduced to SAP HANA and basic database concepts, helping me understand how SAP applications manage and process large amounts of business data efficiently.

## Skills I Built

![Some code we were writing!](/images/blog/2026-10-15-bootcamp-of-sap-2.webp "Some code we were writing!")

- ABAP Programming
- Object-Oriented ABAP (OOABAP)
- SAP HANA Fundamentals
- SAP Reports & Internal Tables
- Basic Enterprise Application Development

## Overall Experience

![Last day at Techno India for the bootcamp!](/images/blog/2026-10-15-bootcamp-of-sap-3.webp "Last day at Techno India for the bootcamp!")

The bootcamp was a great learning experience and gave me a strong foundation in SAP technologies. It helped me improve my programming skills, understand enterprise-level software systems, and gain practical exposure to how real-world business applications are developed.
