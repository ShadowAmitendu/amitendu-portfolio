There is something genuinely nice about a website that does one thing well. No infinite feeds, no popups, no algorithmic notifications — just a quiet little page crafted by hand that says hello.

> "A personal website is not just content; it is a small room on the internet that belongs entirely to you."

This site is written in Markdown and statically generated with Eleventy and Tailwind CSS. I hope to write more small notes and essays here soon. Welcome to my web log!
